# House rules for Mr. Daniel's student-facing tools

These apply to every build in this folder. The build brief (`FIFO_Tool_Build_Brief.md`) is the source of truth for content and wording.

## How to work
- Before coding, read the brief and the existing code, then ask any questions in one batch.
- Build a small slice first (Add a purchase + the pipe), run it, and stop for review before building the rest.
- Don't add features the brief doesn't ask for. Suggest them in chat instead.
- Run the checks in the brief yourself before saying something works. Check at 375px, 768px and 1280px wide.
- When a comment from Dan is a change of mind (not a bug), update the brief first, then the code.

## Students
- Secondary 3 and 4 POA students in Singapore, G2 and G3, mixed ability, on iPads, Chromebooks and phones.
- Short, direct text: no more than about 60 words of instruction before an interaction.
- Every interaction gets a one-line instruction. Error messages name the exact problem and how to fix it.

## Design
- Light, minimal, high-contrast theme; readable in black-and-white print.
- Clean sans-serif font. No monospace, including for numbers.
- Nothing pre-filled or pre-selected.
- Clear gaps between blocks (40 to 48px); buttons side by side with a gap of at least 12px.
- Taps and clicks only (no hover-only features).
- No teacher-only text on student screens.

## Language and POA conventions (2026 MOE/SEAB syllabus)
- Write "and", never "&".
- Terms: Inventory, Trade Payables, Cash at bank, Sales Revenue, Cost of Sales, Ending Inventory Balance, Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Financial Performance.
- FIFO takes whole batches only; no unit costs and no partial-batch apportionment.
- Journals: Particulars, Dr and Cr columns; the credit account is indented on the next line.
